## Analysis: was there any signal below the detection threshold?

`tools/analyze-pass.mjs` fitted the expected pass shape to all 1,357 logged readings (2:47 to 3:54 PM, one every
2 to 3 s). The shape is the received power from free-space loss along the real pass, scaled to 1 at the 62 deg peak.
The fit also allows a constant and a slow drift. Then the same fit was repeated with the pass shape moved to 26
times when no satellite was up, to see how big a bump noise alone makes.

| Reading used | Fitted bump at the peak | Bumps from noise alone (26 windows) | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filtered (`rfFiltDbm`, the fit includes the Kalman lag) | **-0.007 dB** | mean -0.011, spread 0.068 dB | 0.1 sigma |
| Raw (`rfRawDbm`) | -0.075 dB | mean -0.029, spread 0.056 dB | -0.8 sigma |

**Result: no measurable signal. Any bump from the satellite at the 62 deg peak was below about 0.13 dB** (twice the
noise-window spread, about 95% confidence). Caveat: the 26 no-satellite windows overlap (they slide 30 s at a time
through about 50 minutes of quiet data), so that spread is approximate.

To rerun it: `node tools/analyze-pass.mjs docs/validation/data/2026-11-01 2026-11-01T20:14:25Z 2026-11-01T20:29:41Z`

## Why: link budget at the peak

| Term | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Satellite EIRP | +33.2 to +38.0 dBm | Assumed: published LRPT range, 2 to 6.3 W |
| Free-space loss, 915 km at 137.9 MHz | 134.5 dB | Computed (Friis) |
| Polarization (circular to linear) | 3 dB | Standard |
| Antenna gain toward 62 deg, mismatch, coax | about 0 dBi, 0.2 dB, 0.3 dB | Assumed; mismatch from the measured SWR 1.5 |
| **Signal at the LNA input** | **about -102 dBm** (-107 to -98) | |
| Thermal noise in about 3 MHz at about 575 K | about -106 dBm | Assumed bandwidth and sky temperature |

- **If the detector saw only amplified thermal noise**, the signal would raise it by about +5.6 dB (+1 to +10 dB).
  That would have been detected easily. **The measurement rules this case out.**
- **The detector actually reads about -58 dBm**, about 18 dB more than amplified thermal noise should give (about
  -76 dBm with about 30 dB of LNA gain, assumed). The ADC counts (351) correspond to about 0.65 V at the AD8307,
  which really is about -58 dBm within the chip's +-5 dB intercept tolerance, so this is real power, not a
  conversion error. With that floor the satellite adds only about +0.2 dB (+0.1 to +1.0), at or below the limit
  measured above.

**Likely cause (not yet confirmed):** the AD8307 measures everything from DC to 500 MHz. Either broadband noise
from the LNA's last stage (after its SAW filter), or strong signals outside 137 MHz (FM broadcast, for example),
fill that window. The 50 ohm load test tells the two apart:

- the floor stays near -58 dBm with the load on: the noise is made inside the chain; fix with a 137 MHz band-pass
  filter between the SAWbird and the AD8307;
- the floor drops a lot: outside signals; fix with a band-stop filter (for example 88 to 108 MHz) in front of the LNA.

Either would bring the floor toward about -76 dBm, where the same pass should rise by about 3 to 6 dB.
